PLATEFIND
The game

Fifty states, one windshield, however long the drive takes.

The license plate game is older than the interstates. You are in the car, someone spots an out-of-state plate, and the hunt is on: find one from every single state before the trip ends. Almost nobody finishes. That is the point.

PlateFind is the scorecard. It holds all fifty states plus the District of Columbia, remembers what you have already claimed, and tells you what is actually printed on each plate — so the argument about whether Wyoming has a cowboy on it can be settled at seventy miles an hour.

It keeps working with no bars

The plate list is saved to your device the first time it loads. Out past the last cell tower, PlateFind still opens, still checks states off, and quietly catches up the next time you have signal. The header always says which mode you are in.
