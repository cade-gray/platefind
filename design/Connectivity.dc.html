Signal, and the lack of it

The header chip is the single place the app admits what it knows. Four states, one line of copy each.
Checking off plates works in all four
Default
Online

Signal is good and the plate list matches the server. No banner, no fuss — the chip is the only thing on screen that mentions the network at all.

First load, or a refresh
Updating list…

The saved list is already on screen and fully usable while the new one downloads in the background. Nothing blocks, nothing spins in the middle of the page.

No signal
Offline — cached

Amber, not red: this is an expected condition on a road trip, not a failure. A one-line banner under the header says when the list was saved and confirms that finds are being kept on the device.

No signal, old cache
List from 12 Jun

Once the saved list is more than a month old the chip names the date instead of saying “offline”. New plate designs may be missing; the game still counts.

No signal — showing the list saved today at 2:14 PM.
The banner

Appears once, directly under the header, and stays until signal returns. It never covers content and is never dismissible — if it is gone, you are online.
